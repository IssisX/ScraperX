## The story

A big weight is already falling on a cable. Each time it passes a stop, a real gate on the next band starts to close. You can see the next gate move. You can slow the weight. You cannot stop it. When a gate closes, that band is harder, not gone: a wreck still leaves a way.

The Matron was the night nurse who stayed when the crew left. She treats the building, and everyone still in it, as her ward. She is why you cannot take your time on the jobs. She keeps a chart. The pages are right.

A few crew are still alive on the way up. One of them has a radio. It only works while a machine you can reach is feeding it. He is the one who knows the top job.

The only way out is the top. Seat the last beam. Light the beacon by landing on the plate. Run the arm. Open the parachute. If the last gate takes the runway before you jump, the arm is still there and the way out is not. You are in the building with her.

That is the job. That is the clock. That is the finish.
