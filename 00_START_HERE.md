## 1. What the game is

A first-person 3D ascent through an open industrial megastructure, with an unbroken physical route from ground through hundreds of metres and onward beyond 1,000 m toward the existing 1,600 m tower. Huge exposed height, understandable heavy cause and effect, satisfying danger and varied physical movement are central; the environment is the main game system. The [dated owner charter in the Atlas](01_PRODUCT_AUTHORITY/02_ASCENT_ATLAS.md#1-content-decision) records the latest direction alongside the existing [Governing Laws](01_PRODUCT_AUTHORITY/00_GOVERNING_LAWS.md) and [GDD](01_PRODUCT_AUTHORITY/01_SCRAPERX_GDD.md).

Native C++17/Jolt owns consequential geometry, collision, forces, constraints, momentum, torque, moving supports and mechanical state; Godot 4.7 owns touch input, presentation, camera, HUD and Android delivery. Jump, vault, mantle, hang, shimmy, crouch and moving-support traversal combine with large visible counterweights, racks, pulleys, pivots, gears, cranes, linkages, catches, lifts and hinges that actually create paths. Avoid repetitive machinery, regular ramp/stair ascent and tiny unreadable generic obstacles. No progress teleport, invisible blocker, fake route flag or scripted substitute may manufacture physical success. Explicit development/test restarts remain authorized non-progress tools.

The earlier connected-route milestone was grade→300 m. The current owner goal remains ascent beyond **1,000 m** toward the **1,600 m** tower destination. AS-023's +352 m receiving range is an interim opening transfer, one part of a larger game with further parkour and dynamic mechanisms around/above its landing range. GDD §16 owns creative encounter criteria, Laws §15 owns the causal-state rule, and the execution protocol owns how work and evidence are handled.
