A fixed-capacity receiver patch now records only coincident same-body top leaves with matching normals. Plan acceptance still requires a fresh actual solved bottom contact in that exact set, and acquisition still uses the actual world material point and accepted footprint. Raised, sloped, neighboring and overflow candidates reject; prospective geometry cannot start a foot row.

Only slingshot worlds register the collision-step listener. Other worlds stepped hands before Jolt Update but never stepped the finite foot actuator. The existing foot pre-step law now has a shared helper invoked by the listener in substepped worlds and once before Update otherwise. These paths are exclusive. Force, work, stroke, duration, spring and damping laws are unchanged.
