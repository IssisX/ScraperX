# Forklift-built loose-beam ramp

The existing600kg stock beam2446 can form a ramp onto a new braced receiving
trestle1491, west of the freight-yard workshop. The beam keeps its original
mass, shape, friction and six degrees of freedom. The receiving trestle is
fixed industrial structure; it creates no attachment or placement state.

## Interaction

Approach the forklift from normal yard arrival and board its real rear
platform. Use the existing drive, steering, lift and tilt controls. Pick up
the beam broadside, travel with it low, align beside the receiving trestle,
raise it clear of the2.4m deck and lower one end onto the deck. Lower the
empty forks enough to release the beam, while keeping them clear of the
original loose timber blocks, then reverse out.

The player can leave the truck, jump onto the low end, walk up the actual
beam and onto the receiving deck, move onward and drop back to grade.
There is no claim that this optional yard practice route is the only way
to reach the deck. The view uses the same native Kit geometry and labels
the deck LIFT · PLACE · CLIMB.

## Causal boundaries

All cargo handling uses existing fork contact and finite forklift
actuators. No cargo parenting, fixed joint, hidden bearing, snap, gravity
change, direct body-velocity command or revised force limit was added.
The beam is independently supported after the forks withdraw. A bad
placement may slide or fall. Removing its support does not preserve a
fictional completed route.

Controls, timing and authored layout may favor readable, enjoyable play.
The owner's arcade-feel correction does not authorize fake load support
or erase tipping and contact consequences.
