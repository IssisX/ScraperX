Taut extension uses a finite unilateral implicit radial spring/dashpot impulse with sum(w^2 inverse_vertex_mass) plus the actual anchor's frame translation/rotation inverse mass. Transpose weights apply the cloth impulse; equal/opposite reaction and real anchor lever torque update cached frame velocity and buffered native writeback. Readbacks: tether_material_endpoint(), tether_anchor_endpoint(), tether_tension(), tether_elastic_energy(), tether_dissipated_work(). Endpoints and elastic energy derive actual current native state. Tension is the last native row's impulse/dt. Work is accumulated discrete radial predictor kinetic-plus-elastic loss, including implicit numerical loss; it is not a scene energy closure or merely c*v^2 command estimate. Restore resets transient tension/work and leaves the existing native capture byte format unchanged.
