The author describes a concrete brick dropped four feet onto asphalt,
recorded with an AT897 shotgun microphone and denoised in iZotope RX 11.

`843338_16682330-hq.mp3` is the unchanged public high-quality preview.
`concrete_break_denoised_843338_preview.wav` is decoded from that MP3 preview;
it is not the original 24-bit WAV advertised on the source page. The derivative
is signed 16-bit PCM, mono, 44100 Hz, 111028 samples (2.517641723356009 seconds).

Conversion used FFmpeg 8.0.1-3ubuntu2 in the existing Ubuntu proot container,
with no gain adjustment, normalization or trimming. The source was already
mono at 44100 Hz. `provenance.json` records the command, hashes, byte counts
and measured format. No subjective audition or audio-quality claim is made.
