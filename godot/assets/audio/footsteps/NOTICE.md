# Recorded footsteps

The twelve real single-step recordings comprise four recorded hard-surface Foley takes by Fantozzi, four metal takes by Eelke via congusbongus, and four gravel takes by Ali_6868 via the same collection. Their licenses are CC0 and CC BY 3.0 as detailed in [NOTICE.txt](NOTICE.txt), including the original metal page’s current CC BY 4.0 listing.

The canonical notice is included in Android exports and displayed in the existing AUDIO settings page. `provenance.json` records source/archive/output hashes, exact selected filenames and processing. Whole clips are decoded/resampled to mono 22,050 Hz 16-bit PCM WAV and peak-normalized to −1.5 dBFS; no additional cropping, fades, compression or EQ. Godot WAV imports retain `compress/mode=0`.
