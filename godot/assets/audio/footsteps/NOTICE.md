# Recorded footsteps

These twelve step clips are edited from real boot recordings published on Freesound under CC0 1.0 Universal. The source pages mark each sound CC0, which permits copying, modification and commercial distribution without asking permission. Credit is not required; the creators are named here and in `provenance.json`.

- Concrete boots: “concrete_17a_boots_walk.wav” by **sturmankin**, https://freesound.org/people/sturmankin/sounds/272253/
- Boots on metal: “Steps boots on metal surface” by **RFCaldas**, https://freesound.org/people/RFCaldas/sounds/749711/
- Soil boots: “soil_17a_boots_walk.wav” by **sturmankin**, https://freesound.org/people/sturmankin/sounds/278913/

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The edits use each source's public HQ MP3 preview, cut to one recorded footfall, converted to mono 22,050 Hz 16-bit PCM WAV, peak-normalized to −1.5 dBFS, and given 4 ms fade-ins and 18 ms fade-outs. Source preview URLs, trim offsets and output SHA-256 values are recorded in `provenance.json`. The full source recordings are not included.

Godot WAV import settings are committed for these clips with `compress/mode=0`, preserving the 16-bit PCM playback resources. Other audio import settings are unchanged.
