Five separate takes were trimmed at silence boundaries, converted to mono
44.1 kHz Vorbis, high-pass filtered at 65 Hz, and normalized to -18 LUFS
with a -2 dB true-peak target. No synthetic voice or imitation is used.
The edit intervals and resulting file hashes are in provenance.json.

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each added entry in `provenance.json` records the source page, exact downloaded
HQ preview, source/output SHA-256, trim interval, processing, and measured
decoded loudness/true peak. These are MP3-preview-derived human recordings,
not lossless source downloads. Added clips use two-pass -18 LUFS / -2 dBTP
normalization, mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis, a 65 Hz high-pass and short edge fades.
Trims were inspected with silence detection and decoded signal measurements;
subjective listening or device-speaker quality is not claimed.

Alarm and panic each use an independent shuffled bag: every take plays once
before the pool refills, with no identical take at a cycle boundary. Native
fall thresholds, cancellation, cooldown, episode limits and Effects/Master
routing remain the playback authority.
