# Falling voice recordings

Human performances by Fabrizio84, released under CC0 1.0 on Freesound:

- “Screaming Fuck”: https://freesound.org/people/Fabrizio84/sounds/457964/
- “Me Saying WTF”: https://freesound.org/people/Fabrizio84/sounds/458000/

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source and license checked 2026-09-26. Source MP3 previews:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/457/457964_2841496-hq.mp3
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/458/458000_2841496-hq.mp3

Five separate takes were trimmed at silence boundaries, converted to mono
44.1 kHz Vorbis, high-pass filtered at 65 Hz, and normalized to -18 LUFS
with a -2 dB true-peak target. No synthetic voice or imitation is used.
The edit intervals and resulting file hashes are in provenance.json.
