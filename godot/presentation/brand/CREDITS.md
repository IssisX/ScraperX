# The launcher icon and brand art: credits

Drawn by the project's own code, `godot/tools/generate_icon.gd` (run it to
redraw them): a tapering steel lattice tower, cross-braced, in the HUD's
amber on its ink, with one lamp at the top. No text, so nothing depends on
the store name. Deterministic: the same script draws the same pixels.
