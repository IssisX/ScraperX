SCRAPERX STORY RADIO VOICES: NOTICES

The story radio's remade lines (listed in CREDITS.md) were generated offline
by src/voice/generate_story_voice.py with Chatterbox TTS. Each speaker's voice
comes from a reference passage spoken by a Piper voice. Whisper checked every
take; nothing of Whisper or Piper ships in the game. The lines not yet remade
are Kokoro-82M takes, under KOKORO_LICENSE.txt.

Speaker  Piper reference voice           Its training data
IONA     en_US-ljspeech-high             LJ Speech (Keith Ito, Linda Johnson), public domain
RO       en_US-john-medium               LibriVox recordings, public domain
         (also the terminal's RECORD and SOURCE playback)
ARI      en_US-bryce-medium              Bryce Beattie's own voice, public domain
DEV      en_GB-vctk-medium, speaker p227 CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92, CC BY 4.0
