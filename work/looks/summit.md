# Look rubric: the summit ending (touch_summit_ending)

Frames: `screenshots/summit_ending_<beat>.png` for the five beats below, captured at 1296x1114 under
xvfb (`gl_compatibility`, 60 fps fixed) by the proof step "Render the complete summit ending through
touch", which plays the ending through the real touch path. Written 2026-11-02 from trunk's frames.

Every line must hold in its frame.

1. `hanging_beam`: first person at the summit steelwork under open sky, the last beam (yellow) hanging
   overhead, and the touch action `LOWER LAST BEAM` showing beside `JUMP` and `CROUCH`.
2. `seated_foreman`: the beam is seated and the foreman's radio line is on screen as a subtitle with
   its speaker label (`... ON THE RADIO`); the action now reads `FREE SLING`.
3. `city`: the view from the top: the deck runs ahead to open air, and the far world (mountains,
   horizon, sky) fills the distance with no void or missing ground.
4. `parachute`: her own two arms reach up to the risers in first person, the canopy banner
   (`CANOPY DEPLOYED`) and the altitude readout show, the `STOW` action replaces the jump, and the
   ground lies far below.
5. `home`: the closing card: the title `SCRAPERX`, the line `THE LAST BEAM IS HOME. SO ARE YOU.` and
   `A game by Cory`, centered and readable over the dim landscape.
6. In every frame: the touch controls (stick at the lower left, the action buttons at the lower right,
   pause at the top left) lie inside the frame, do not overlap one another, and their labels are
   readable; no surface shows a missing-texture color (bright magenta or checkerboard).
