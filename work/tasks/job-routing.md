# Job routing: Muse or a Claude helper (11-12)

Answer these when you write or assign a job. Muse is free, so it keeps every job these do not send away.
Data: 49 Muse jobs, 10-29 to 11-12. A failed try is a red proof of its own or a fix it said did not work.

## The questions
1. Does the job name the cause, or the exact way to build it (the function, the numbers, the spec)?
2. Must several numbers settle together to pass (gains, timing, a swing, a balance, one bar two things must clear)?
3. Does it change what she spends from her one 8 kN / 44 kW account?
4. Does it touch two or more game systems (her mind or route, his movement, bodies and contacts, a machine,
   world geometry, gains)?
5. Does it build on a job that has not landed?

## Where it goes
- Q2 yes, or Q1 no with Q3 or Q4 yes: a Claude helper (CLAUDE.md's ladder: Opus high first) finds the cause
  or the numbers first; Muse then builds what the helper names.
- Q5 yes: hold it until the other job lands, or give both to one owner. Never stack it on an open branch.
- Q1 no, but one system and no tuning: Muse, with Grok's math or ChatGPT's research asked at the start.
- Q1 yes: Muse, even when it is protected, large or crosses systems.
- Always Muse: tooling and CI, presentation in one system, a move with its spec numbers given.
- Time (Cory 11-12, "if anything at all is going to take an estimated week from muse - YOU DO IT"): a job
  Muse would take about a week (Q1 no with Q3 or Q4 yes: 72 h median and open jobs run longer) goes to a helper
  at assignment, and any Muse job held 3 days is re-checked and taken.

## The counts (jobs; mean failed tries; share with 2 or more)
- Q1: named 30; 0.3; 3% (5 h median to land). Not named 19; 4.2; 95% (13 still open).
- Q2: yes 9; 5.8; 100% (all 9 still open). No 40; 0.9; 25%.
- Q3: yes 7; 5.0; 86%. No 42; 1.3; 31%.
- Q4: two or more 22; 3.1; 64%. One 27; 0.7; 19%. With the cause named: 7; 0.1; 0%.
- Q5: yes 4; 4.8; 100%.
- Send-away rule above: 15; 4.5; 93% (12 open). The rest: 34; 0.6; 15%.
- Not named, one system, no tuning: 4; 2.8; 100%, but 3 landed (72 h median).
- Protected: 23; 2.6; 57%, but with the cause named 11; 0.5; 9%. Size follows the same way.
- Clean for Muse: CI and tooling 6; 0.3. One-system presentation 7; 0.6; 14%.

## Re-check a Muse job while it runs
Move it to a helper (mail Muse "taking it") the moment one of these turns true:
- a red shows in a test group or branch the change does not reach (19 jobs: 3.9; 84%);
- the cause turns out to cross systems, or Q2 or Q3 turns yes;
- Muse asks "stuck", "ruling needed" or "which is it" twice on the same failure;
- its second fix for the same failure fails (the 11-12 rule).
Moving is no cure: J-001 stayed red through five helper passes. The helper names the cause; Muse builds.
