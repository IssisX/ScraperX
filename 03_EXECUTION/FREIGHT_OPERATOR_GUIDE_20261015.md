# Forklift and yard crane — play guide

The warehouse truck is west of the tower. Walk onto its rear platform and use the contextual action to operate it. Your viewpoint stays at the actual operator position; look around independently while driving or using the crane.

| Forklift action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forward / neutral / reverse | F / N / R | RB / Y / LB |
| Accelerator / service brake | W / S or Space | RT / LT |
| Rear-wheel steering | A / D | Left stick |
| Raise / lower forks | Up / Down | D-pad Up / Down |
| Tilt back / forward | Left / Right | D-pad Left / Right |
| Inching / fine lift and tilt | Hold Shift | Hold right-stick press |
| Couple / uncouple original forks | G | View / Back |
| Parking brake | P | Left-stick press |
| Horn | H | A |
| Leave controls | E or Q | X or B |

Touch shows separate direction, brake, lift, tilt, inching and horn controls. Heavy loads and their distance from the front axle affect real stability. The accepted1700kg near/far test tips and recovers the truck through actual load position; no upright rescue holds it level.

The fork parking cradle is the pair of low steel rails east/south of the starting truck, near(-88,-70) in yard coordinates. Approach with raised forks facing south. Level the actual blades, lower gently onto the rails, select PARK and release the hydraulic levers. Use G, View or the dedicated touch COUPLE/UNCOUPLE button only when the native panel reports ready. The mast-angle readout is relative to the pitched truck; look at the blades against the rails. Hold INCH to slow lift and tilt for placement. If the panel says RELIEVE COUPLER LOAD, stop pushing down and feather up. A rejected request expires; ask again after correcting the condition. The same loose forks stay on the stand while you withdraw, then return slowly with matching carriage height/tilt to recouple. Pause or leaving controls cancels pending intent.

For freight transfer, drive the original truck to the yard crane pickup. The four slings attach to four actual chassis eyes. Park and stow the truck with forks clear of the ground, leave its platform, and walk to each eye. The contextual action offers RIG LEG or UNRIG LEG for the eye you can physically reach. Keyboard G rigs and R unrigs; on foot gamepad X uses the contextual action and Y requests unrigging. Touch uses the contextual button. Alignment, slow relative movement, clear cable paths and supported truck geometry govern acceptance. Any attached leg interlocks truck drive, lift and tilt.

Walk onto the crane's real booth floor and choose ENTER CAB.

| Crane action | Keyboard | Gamepad / touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slew | A / D | Left stick horizontal |
| Trolley inward / outward | S / W | Left stick vertical |
| Hoist up / down | Up / Down | RT / LT; touch hoist buttons |
| Look around | Mouse | Right stick / right touch area |
| Leave controls | E or Q | X or B / DONE |

Release the controls to return the machine targets to neutral. Pause and leaving the station clear held commands. The panel reports attached legs, actual line force/length, slew, trolley and remaining hoist energy. Gloves and lever motion follow accepted native machine rates, and the three machine sounds follow their respective native effort receipts.

Lift the entire truck above the receiving dock's side rails before slewing across them. The first freight dock meets the existing11m ring. Land on all three tires and pay out enough line to make every sling genuinely slack. Leave the booth and use the existing cargo-net personnel route to reach the ring, then walk to the dock and unrig each local eye. A suspended or tensioned leg refuses release. Reboard the same truck to drive through the dock mouth onto the ring; the truck can reverse back to the dock for another operation.
