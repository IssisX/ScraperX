## Locked direction

The player completes physically broken or unlinked machinery, rides its consequence
and uses the structure between stages. Neighboring outputs must matter: displaced
mass, available water, changed load paths or reachable leftover structure. Stage
inputs must be matched to actual producer outputs, not merely narrated as a cascade.

Rearming follows physics. A source can be used again only when material survives,
a reachable mechanism can replace its energy, and the full load/unload/control
cycle closes. Destructive sources are one-shot and leave real aftermath; that
wreckage is a route only when the existing controller can traverse its geometry.

Keep the independent tower stair. AS-004/005 may later provide optional useful
freight/structural routes; they are not prerequisites to 154 m. No proven route
is removed to make a machine necessary. The first upper band remains 154→220 m;
Atlas band cuts and the 1600 m summit condition remain unchanged.

## Energy and contacts govern the design

A typical upper lift gains roughly 10–60 m; this is an authoring scale, not a
minimum that forbids a shorter useful seam. Work backward from a stable exit to
transfer, payload, transmission, source and player-accessible trigger; validate
forward through the actual runtime. Use the least complexity that buys gameplay,
physical causality or structural value. Static industrial parkour can be correct.

Gravity-driven sources must lose enough gravitational potential to pay the lifted
payload, kinetic energy and losses. They need not all be above the payload; actual
centres of mass and displacements determine work. Springs, pressure and powered
screws require their own charged energy/input-work accounts, not the false rule
that “energy only flows downhill.” The ground water screw uses finite motor work;
the downstream bucket spends stored potential. Never count the same work twice.

“No link, no lift” means no useful payload rise without a real energy transmission.
An unlinked source may settle, hit a catch, dissipate energy or become spent. Name
and test that state. AS-006's bollard arrests about 3 cm of motion; it does not
preserve every joule in an unmoving source.

Connections are actual contacts, joints, anchors and force/flow laws. Scripted
native reduced-order physics is legitimate when it owns the relevant quantities
and is validated; a mission/completion flag cannot substitute for transmission.
Compatible authored anchors are implemented; “any anchor in the building” is a
product ambition, not arbitrary mesh attachment already available.

Rider motion follows `v + ω×r`. Normal braking/landing must remain controllable
and stable. Physically credible missed contacts may cause falls; an explosive
solver arrest or unjustified velocity erasure is a defect, not desirable physics.

## Build and acceptance boundary

Ground mechanism rehabilitation precedes more upper construction; the current
start-page ledger records its successful proof and remaining observation gaps.
Do not infer authorization for another ascent from this standing direction.

When upper work is authorized, close the smallest outstanding receiving seam in
AS-006 before extending it. Add only the movement/rigging/flow capability that seam
actually needs. Do not schedule the entire aspirational move set as a compulsory
prerequisite to every lift. Each implementation ends with actual stable support,
recovery and a coherent saved state, without depending on a later unfinished stage.

The whole band requires a normal 154→220 linked route, an independent climbing
route, the claimed neighbor transfer/rearm, and equivalent checkpoint continuation.
Each is separately unproven until exercised. One green cage ride does not certify
the band, every structural rating, or every legal sequence break.

Above 220 m, AS-007 remains blocked; later tickets are unauthored. Re-derive their
modules against the actual world before coding. No band load may reset useful
bodies or inventories. Reduced/sleeping representations must retain equivalent
consequences, and real-device performance remains a measured shipping requirement.
