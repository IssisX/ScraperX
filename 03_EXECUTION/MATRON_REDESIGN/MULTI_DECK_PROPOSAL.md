## Recommended cohesive plan

Use **a small directed route graph over real supports, executed by a finite-force climbing controller**, with machinery as separately qualified shortcuts. The graph design supplies connectivity and evidence handling; the holds design supplies the physical executor and the most decisive early tests; the machinery design supplies passenger-state checks, spent-machine handling, and capacity skepticism. Do not make autonomous machine operation the first dependency: it would combine climbing, rigging, load certification, passenger stability, control access, and persistence before proving the basic pursuit loop.

In plain English: she learns routes through steel that is already there, but she still has to reach it, hold her own weight, fit through the opening, and get her feet onto the next deck. If the route is blocked or she loses her grip, she reacts to that real problem. She does not appear on another floor because the planner chose it.

### 2. Physical climbing contract

Introduce one controller with `Approach → Probe → Mount → Transfer → Dismount → Supported`, plus `Retreat`, `Slip` and `Fall`. Transitions depend on measured support/contact and relative motion. Arrival requires contact on the destination footprint for a short declared settling interval, not only matching its Y coordinate. Establish the interval from fixed-step tests; do not conceal an ungrounded arrival with a long delay.

Reuse geometric leaf-shape extraction from [src/sim/physics_world_climb.cpp:25–89](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world_climb.cpp#L25-L89), and the capped-force/reaction pattern from [src/sim/physics_world_grab.cpp:323–334](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world_grab.cpp#L323-L334). Do not reuse the player's complete controller, alter player reach/grip constants, or treat Kit's welded dynamic-load grip as a static rung hand. Previous-branch physical ladder code is design input only: port the minimum verified mechanism, reconcile its body/hand assumptions with current capture, and retain current fatality work.

Start with two **massless compliant hand contacts** and the existing body, rather than new hand rigid bodies or a whole-body articulated rig. This is an explicit reduced model: hand targets approach at finite speed, contact forces are applied at declared shoulder/contact locations, damping uses relative support-point velocity, and no hard positional constraint closes an offset. Acquisition cannot inject elastic energy for free; account for spring energy and target changes. Use a bounded candidate query parameterized by actor shape, reach, excluded entities and member identity; do not copy the player-specific fallback that assigns both hands to one hold, its slimmed sweep, or its unbounded all-hit collector ([src/sim/physics_world_climb.cpp:25–89](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world_climb.cpp#L25-L89), [src/sim/physics_world_climb.cpp:111–139](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world_climb.cpp#L111-L139)). Each contact must identify reachable real geometry, have a finite closure speed, unobstructed shoulder-to-hand access, admissible load directions, and capped compliant force. Rungs can provide a hook-like contact; a smooth vertical pole needs finite normal squeeze and tangential friction. A flange may support a real foot/contact; its arithmetic height cannot stop a falling body. Apply reaction at the contact point on dynamic supports; record the world reaction for static supports. Record reaction force and moment at both modeled endpoints; unbalanced moments cannot disappear into the rotation lock. Any configuration requiring an unmodeled stabilizing couple remains unqualified. Torque fidelity is limited by the current translation-only body and must be stated, not claimed solved. Compute the required support/contact wrench before qualification; reject unsupported off-center hangs and narrow-board/shimmy configurations whose stabilizing moment cannot be supplied by the modeled admissible contacts. Surviving because rotation is locked is not balance evidence. The home ladder clear rail interior is about 0.50 m, narrower than her 0.60 m capsule, so qualification must demonstrate a swept face-climb and above-rail mantle, not a centerline corridor.

For the first experiment, use one explicit parameter set rather than blending all authors' suggestions: **3.5 kN per hooked hand, 5 kN combined effort (sum of contact-force magnitudes, not the norm of their canceling vector sum), 1.5 kW positive mechanical power, 180 kJ positive-work reserve**, all **proposed calibration limits**, not established biological or structural capacities. Body mass is 220 kg, so static weight is about 2.16 kN and a 22.25 m rise costs at least 48 kJ of gravitational work. Include stored compliant energy and actuator work in the ledger; moving-support energy must not be charged or credited twice. Use **1.5 kW active negative-power and 60 kJ accumulated active dissipation** as initial experimental braking limits, with separately measured bounded compliance/impact energy. Active braking consumes a persisted thermal allowance, recovering at a proposed maximum 1 kJ/s only on genuine supported rest; P0 starts with a declared allowance, and repeated-descent tests must measure that cooling before claiming repeatability. These are conservative test parameters, not established capacities; record any deliberate revision. Do not allow unlimited descent arrest or refill ascent reserve from negative work. Measure per-contact force/impulse and actuator power in the relative attachment frame, plus world gravitational/kinetic energy and solver correction residual, so opposing hands and moving supports cannot hide energy creation. Finite is not automatically stable: force reduction, load, time-step, and contact-loss falsifiers decide whether this model is useful.

Before a transfer, require enough modeled effort for the next verified rest or retreat. Sustained isometric grip must also have an effort-duration limit; a pure positive-work reserve would otherwise allow endless hanging. Proposed normalized effort is the time integral of the sum of each hand load divided by its force cap, with a 60 full-load-second capacity; it cannot decrease while hands carry her unsupported weight. Begin with 0.25 full-load-second recovery per second on genuine foot support, and 1 kJ/s positive-work reserve recovery there. Treat these as calibration assumptions and test exhaustion rather than enlarging them to force a pass. Recovery occurs only on genuine supported rest with a stated bounded rate. Depleting effort causes slip/release if support cannot be maintained; it does not grant extra strength because the player is escaping. If tuning cannot support the body within the chosen limits, revise the model openly or disable that connector.

All new traversal keeps gravity and normal collision active. The old corner-pipe code and its `kOnSteel` path are not fallback executors. Downclimbing requires separately verified mounting, braking, lower holds and feet placement. Missing rungs may be bypassed only through another reachable real contact. An attempted catch must respect closure time and the kinetic energy/impulse that can be dissipated. Mantling ends when the feet actually take the load. Static collision members are treated as the existing rigid world, not newly certified structures; no structural breakage simulation or LOAD-BEARING work is proposed here.

### 3. Pursuit, investigation and recovery loop

A sight/sound creates a spatial lead with source time, confidence, region hypothesis and any observed exit. If no region matches, retain an uncertain spatial lead; do not default it to her current floor. Reuse bounded sound inputs and current stage behavior. Register actual openings for the first territory and replace its fixed-shaft acoustic assumptions with bounded geometric path candidates; do not simulate acoustic waves or add an all-tower acoustic graph. Deck occlusion and narrowing-shaft tests must precede expanding that territory. Sounds from outside her travel territory can be heard without implying a usable route.

Plan toward a supported investigation location. Walk to a connector, validate current clearance and holds, traverse, settle, listen, and continue. A changed lead may redirect the remaining route, but cannot instantly reverse velocity, release an essential grip or reverse a running machine. A failed approach blocks that direction/state, not every route to the destination. Reinspect on a relevant support/machine change; at most two unchanged retries, then choose another supported investigation point or rest. A cooldown alone cannot repeatedly refresh the same stale lead.

Proposed initial lease: predicted qualified travel time plus 12 seconds of local inspection, capped at **180 seconds since the last real sensory evidence**. Progress does not reset evidence age. On expiry while exposed, seek the next physically reachable rest/retreat; do not prolong target pursuit indefinitely. At rest, complete the bounded local search and patrol a reachable component. Search wall time advances even when its target is unreachable. A stranded Matron remains in the actual component and listens. New sound must not clear an unchanged geometry block or reset its retry count. There is no navigation respawn. Stage-specific frail/Turn restrictions remain explicit capability predicates, including the existing bay exception, instead of being accidentally removed by a generic graph.

In plain English: she can follow a noisy escape up several decks and come back down after a later sound. Silence can still lose her. She searches where the evidence took her, and a broken route can genuinely leave her on the wrong floor.

### 5. Machinery: retain the useful part, defer the unsupported part

First expose read-only receiver, support, rigging/spent-state and motion observations. A passenger must reach the actual platform, remain supported under acceleration, and physically step onto a receiver. Existing `riding_` behavior is a useful seam, not proof of horizontal/rotating support stability. Include the incremental cost of waking the entire coupled mechanism island in machine budgets; AI-only timing is insufficient. Normal load limits tangential stance force; any rail grip has the same finite contact rules as climbing. No loaded-player rides or climbs in the first rollout.

Autonomous operation is separate from being a passenger. An AI call to an abstract `Action`/travel target is not proof of reaching and operating a real control. Do not add imaginary handles, unlimited motor supplies, free reset energy, magically rearmed rubble, or invisible ropes. A/B/C counterweight systems have different spent/reset states; each direction needs its own physically possible sequence. Existing zero-rated rope behavior cannot certify a finite allowable load. A stopgap “she survived” test is insufficient.

The first optional machine study should be a passenger on an already functioning, physically qualified support, not a mandatory shackle-operation rewrite. The automatic traction tram is a later candidate once a real route reaches its receivers. A skip trial may measure energy and boarding at 154→176.25, but it cannot unlock a production edge until the rope/control/capacity concerns are resolved within separately authorized scope. Current ziplines likewise remain separately gated: their brake cap is not a transverse hand-load cap. No cable descent is needed to make P1 or P2 reversible; the physical climbing route must supply its own returns.

### 6. Capture, fatality and persistence are release gates

Introduce an explicit owner/token for locomotion and hand use **before any pre-step actuator runs**. Current `grab_pre_step` precedes Matron pre-step, followed by Jolt and then grab/Matron post-step ([src/sim/physics_world.cpp:577](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world.cpp#L577), [src/sim/physics_world.cpp:708–767](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world.cpp#L708-L767)); insert arbitration ahead of those force paths, and report arrivals/tears after the solve. Every owner transition cancels stale legacy climb/pipe/foot forces. Capture may begin only from a physically supported eligible state with free required limbs. Eligibility also requires an unobstructed, physically reachable hand approach to the player; distance and height cannot authorize acquisition through a rail or wall. Add a blocked-hand-path negative test for every newly qualified encounter geometry, alongside interrupted-dismount and post-restore capture tests. On capture ownership, routing stops issuing forces. A climbing grip is not discarded to satisfy proximity. Carried-player climbing, midair capture and machine operation while carrying remain disabled as **uncertified actions**, not a proximity nerf to the player.

Do not rewrite or soften the fatality. The current home rig, gore and sequence remain intact. New regions need actual support/rim validation before the existing grab/carry/release path is eligible. The home bollard operation is eligible only when that real rig and an actual supported approach are reachable in the current encounter; no global coordinate drag across floors. If a capture on a new deck cannot complete a validated continuation under current laws, it must not start that continuation. **Expanded hostile-territory release is blocked until a concrete supported capture/recovery behavior is demonstrated there.** P1 can first prove travel in an isolated test, but cannot be reported as a released hostile encounter while this gate is unresolved. Use the current non-rig release/spare path only after that deck's rim/landing physics passes; it is not an automatic substitute or a change to home fatality intensity.

Recovery also needs one targeted correction: current [src/sim/matron.cpp:2270–2279](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L2270-L2279) removes her after falling more than 12 m below her recorded level unless special traversal/riding flags exempt her ([src/sim/matron.hpp:299](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L299)). Add an explicit unsupported-fall ownership state and handoff to actual collision/impact/injury outcomes, preserving real death. Do not retain a fake climb flag indefinitely. Checkpoint eligibility needs its own predicate. Current freezing uses `player_on_deck_` ([src/sim/matron.hpp:882–883](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L882-L883), [src/sim/physics_world.cpp:862](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world.cpp#L862)); do not replace that with “every graph-reachable floor.” Proposed threat predicate: an actual held/capture interaction, current direct perception within an eligible local encounter, or an unexpired pursuit with a presently qualified route and recent physical progress. Test quiet escape, unreachable lead, stalled machinery and committed restoration. Thresholds must be named in the future implementation and tested at boundaries; this proposal grants no new invisible save restriction.

Persist the actual body transform/velocity, movement owner/state, region, route intent, remaining evidence time, effort, contact member/local points, and relevant failure observations. ### 8. Bounded performance, risks and stopping rule

One active Matron; at most **256 regions, 1,024 directed edges, two active hand contacts and four shortlisted hold candidates**. Proposed route/cache memory ceiling **256 KiB**. Index static features by connector/region once; no full-tower scan per tick. Scheduler caps planning at two requests/second and 128 node expansions per fixed step; invalidation queues a bounded request rather than bypassing the cap. Cache adjacency; coalesce invalidations by connector/version, cap invalidation processing at 32 entries per tick, and defer work after budget exhaustion. Use fixed-capacity storage with no allocation in the active traversal path. Bound each speculative shape query to 32 indexed local member candidates before narrow-phase work; an overflow produces unknown/blocked planning evidence and retries from support, never arbitrary omission of safety-critical current contacts. Count candidate visits and collector hits, not just query calls. The 256 KiB ceiling includes graph nodes/edges, queues, caches and observation records; shared immutable geometry and existing Jolt allocations are separately reported, not silently charged elsewhere. Ordinary sensing/probe allowance: at most eight new local geometry queries per fixed step, with only the safety-critical current-contact checks reserved ahead of optional lookahead. Actual pre/post-step contacts and gravity continue at the existing simulation rate.

Performance acceptance is **incremental p95 CPU ≤0.5 ms per simulation tick**, with p99 and worst-case query/solver counts recorded, whole-frame aggregate cost at the actual fixed-step/render ratio, allocations and physics backlog, plus sustained repository-required device performance under pursuit, machinery and capture. The authors' 45 FPS figures are proposed test targets, not observed results or permission to weaken an existing shipping requirement. Record baseline and changed runs on the same device/scenario, including a thermal soak. No offscreen body teleport, active-contact decimation, shared-player-grip nerf, or unlimited invalidation processing is allowed to meet budget. If the cap is missed, reduce speculative planning breadth and cache work before reducing physical fidelity.

Principal risks are finite-contact instability, narrow support/transfer failure, omitted rotational dynamics, body/member identity changes, stale acoustic/region assumptions, checkpoint-format errors, capture state ownership, and hidden machine unlimited constraints. Every one has an explicit experiment or exclusion above. Initial deployment remains a bounded verified territory; keep an auditable coverage table of attempted/passed/blocked directions, not a single “multi-deck works” flag.

Stop this design process after the current review repairs and one source recheck. Additional tournaments do not answer the central unknowns. The next useful evidence is the P0 finite-contact experiment, then the P1 connected round trip—not more speculative route diagrams or an expensive all-tower build.

## Appendix C — Hearing and perception

Pinned-source facts, not a claim that the newer dot hearing work is integrated.

### Verified implementation map

- **The level model is already a vector, not two floors.** [src/sim/matron.cpp:75](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L75) defines deck 154, `:95` ring 176, `:105–123` adds ten upper rings at 22 m spacing (198–396) and thirteen lower decks at 11 m spacing (11–143); bay bracket is another level at `:135–139`. Each owns hand-authored floor lines, loops, optional guard posts and a cage-eye association ([src/sim/matron.hpp:1088–1114](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L1088-L1114)). Upper rings explicitly have no known way up ([matron.cpp:106–116](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L106-L116)). Existence in `levels_` therefore does not prove reachability or sound geometry validity.
- **Floor identity is inferred solely from height.** `floor_of(y)` picks the nearest registered floor with `-0.5 < y-floor < 8`, otherwise returns her current floor ([matron.cpp:350–361](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L350-L361)). No x/z, support-body or tower identity enters this function. `settle_level` uses feet within 0.6 m of a registered floor, skips moving-support rides, and selects the first other matching level (`:675–687`). This is an actual ambiguity for new colocated elevations and a missing-floor fallback, not a demonstrated runtime bug here.
- **Player eligibility and visibility use the first tower footprint.** [matron.cpp:1731–1755](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1731-L1755) defines `abs(x)<27`, `-177<z<-123`, own-feet height window -1..+6, known climb columns within 1 m, and adjacent pipe-connected decks. Visual perception is restricted to on-deck or known climb, then 9 m direct sight / 50 m commitment sight (`:1879–1892`; [matron.hpp:211](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L211),294). `sees` uses two raycasts offset a head-width, ignoring her body and accepting hits within 0.7 m of the target ([matron.cpp:231–254](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L231-L254)). Thus clear visual space elsewhere does not automatically make a player visually eligible. The pipe-related eligibility is broader for `player_on_deck_` than for `perceivable`.
- **Hearing itself is not level/footprint gated.** Player and event sound travel in 3D to body/locker ear ([matron.cpp:1764–1809](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1764-L1809),1883–1898). Full loudness reaches 55 m (`:35–36`), doubled during pipe listening (`:1770`; [matron.hpp:590–591](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L590-L591)). Dormant wake uses a 30 m carried-distance cap ([matron.cpp:1839–1849](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1839-L1849); [matron.hpp:200](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L200)). Straining/emerging advance their sequences without normal hearing/lead handling ([matron.cpp:1852–1870](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1852-L1870)).
- **Acoustics is a chosen approximation, not general geometry propagation.** `decks_between` raycasts all hits and counts only static non-sensor surfaces with vertical normal magnitude >0.7 whose hit height overlaps a registered floor's 0.6 m depth ([matron.cpp:256–298](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L256-L298)). Columns, ramps not matching floor heights, and moving geometry do not muffle as decks. `carry_m` returns straight distance when no such deck crosses; otherwise chooses minimum of straight distance multiplied by 10 per deck and a three-segment path through a fixed shaft box x ±17 / z -150±17, multiplied by 1.4 for each off-shaft end (`:300–322`; [matron.hpp:186–197](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L186-L197)). The header explicitly acknowledges this shaft approximation can pass unmuffled through upper narrowing decks above 264 m. No second-tower/branching-shaft acoustic model is present in these functions.
- **Sound producers and selection are bounded.** Last tick's impacts, hardest first, then yard-crane drives enter `hear`; own thrown-object impact is excluded ([physics_world.cpp:679–691](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world.cpp#L679-L691)). Queue holds 16 events, dropping later ones ([matron.hpp:859–871](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L859-L871)). Events within 1.5 m horizontally / 2 m vertically of her body are excluded as own sound ([matron.cpp:1784–1787](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1784-L1787)), a proximity heuristic rather than source ownership. Selection maximizes loudness / carried distance, then computes attenuated level (`:1773–1801`); the comment “loudest at ear” is therefore not literally maximization of the later linear attenuation formula. Player noise comes from landing speed, grounded speed/crouch/sprint, climbing speed and forklift state ([physics_world_movement.cpp:1218–1256](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world_movement.cpp#L1218-L1256)); bay fan masks locomotion noise, then maximum with room microphone loudness is passed ([physics_world.cpp:701–710](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/physics_world.cpp#L701-L710)). No evidence that every machine in the tower emits a Matron sound was found; the inspected explicit producer loop contains impacts and yard crane only.
- **Lead and exit inference reuse real known route descriptions.** Seen player wins; otherwise selected sound/player noise supplies x/z and inferred floor ([matron.cpp:1900–1937](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1900-L1937)). If seen/heard while riding a zipline, lead becomes its bottom-floor endpoint; on a known climb it becomes climb head/top-out (`:1940–1961`). These are destination guesses, not continued omniscient location tracking. Repeated distractions cause wary inspection on the same level, or after two failures only a head turn while guarding/unrigging (`:1916–1936`).
- **Inter-deck routing is local and height-monotone.** Hunting tries pipe, then descending zipline, then ascending climb ([matron.cpp:2084](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L2084)). Pipe picks one ±11 m step, only between 132 and 154, and only beyond Pest (`:901–937`; [matron.hpp:564](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L564)). Source thus supports 132, 143, 154 despite the start-page shorthand mentioning 143. Zipline selection requires matching top-floor height, a lower destination no lower than the lead, preferring the observed line then nearest ([matron.cpp:690–719](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L690-L719)). No complete inter-level graph search exists in these inspected selectors; routes requiring initial travel away from lead height will not be found by them.
- **Investigation has limits but no unconditional wall-clock expiry.** Ordinary lead expiry is 9 seconds without hearing; reachable-direction pipe lead extends to 40 Dangerous /150 Changed seconds ([matron.cpp:2031–2039](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L2031-L2039); [matron.hpp:213](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L213),581–582). Expired unreachable leads revert to inferred route's departure or projected floor lines on her own level ([matron.cpp:2040–2067](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L2040-L2067)). Search walks toward lead/trail, then increments its 8-second timer only within 1.5 m of lead (`:2143–2165`; [matron.hpp:214](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.hpp#L214)); an unreachable search endpoint does not automatically expire by elapsed world time in this branch. Breadcrumbs are recorded whenever on-deck and body exists, without seeing/hearing requirement (`:1756–1759`), stored by floor and followed only on matching floor ([matron_moves.cpp:100–149](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron_moves.cpp#L100-L149)). This is a designed memory shortcut, not purely sensory trail evidence.
- **Hearing changes physical behavior indirectly.** Attenuated hearing level and spikes feed rage/stage ([matron.cpp:1399–1425](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/27d86a96d89e129a20c10c9298361d6e11581076/src/sim/matron.cpp#L1399-L1425)), which gates pipe eligibility and longer pursuit. Changing acoustics can therefore change movement availability/timing. While bracing the bay load, she still hears and updates rage but suppresses foot/hand pursuit (`:1824–1836`). Moving-support rides also suspend route pursuit (`:1985–1994`). These separations should survive range expansion.

### Integration implications and missing evidence

1. Reuse the current sound/lead pipeline and vector level structure; do not equate a registered floor with traversable access. Connect any new region to authoritative physical supports and real usable route endpoints.
2. A second tower or overlapping deck elevations needs explicit region/support identity for floor, lead, trail and route endpoints; simply adding floors leaves the fixed footprint and y-only fallbacks intact.
3. A taller range must reconcile the acknowledged narrowing-shaft acoustic approximation with actual openings. Otherwise added travel can expose acoustic errors previously excused because she could not reach those rings.
4. New connections must account for local monotone routing, physical stance/approach and failed-route fallback. Merely expanding pipe bounds or climb records is not evidence of successful contact/clearance/top-out.
5. Preserve sensory uncertainty: exit inference from a heard/seen transition, decay, distraction and local investigation. Decide consciously whether unsensed same-deck breadcrumb collection remains acceptable across more decks.
6. Missing validation: irregular floor spacing, sounds >8 m above any mapped floor, same-height regions, second-tower sounds, changing/dynamic deck occlusion, branching routes with height reversal, long quiet multi-hop pursuit, and a search lead unreachable forever. These are missing inspected evidence, not claims that all such tests are absent repository-wide.
