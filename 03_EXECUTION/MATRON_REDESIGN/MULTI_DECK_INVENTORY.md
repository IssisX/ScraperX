## Method and limits

- Body: capsule radius 0.3 m (diameter 0.60 m), height 2.1 m, 220 kg ([matron.hpp:180-184](https://github.com/IssisX/ScraperX/blob/c61811c311f918a1eb52516a5d53b7ab0c4c2033/src/sim/matron.hpp#L180-L184)).
- Rung heights and counts come from replaying each builder's float32 loop. No runtime probe was built and nothing was committed besides these two files.
- *Rail interior clear width* = distance between the inner faces of the stiles. The capsule (0.60 m) is wider than every ladder's interior (0.44 to 0.52 m): she can never pass *between* stiles, so a top-out works only where the stiles stop at the deck top or splay to grab handles (0.84 m clear).
- Status values:
  - **candidate**: geometry admits a 0.30 m / 2.1 m capsule using only walk, step, narrow-board, ladder, top-out mantle, top-entry descent or a machine ride the direction is authored for
  - **blocked**: a concrete reason prevents use (see blocker)
  - **UNVERIFIED**: facts needed to decide were not established
- **UNVERIFIED across the board:** full capsule sweeps (only plan fits were checked), machine load for 220 kg, reverse machine availability, which cable lines actually instantiate, and obstructions from machinery on the rings.
