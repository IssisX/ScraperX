## Research and consequences

Expert movement combines anticipation, contact changes and committed momentum. The useful primate-like qualities are independent hand/foot contacts, overlapping support and deliberate flight. Human movement limits remain binding; siamang morphology is not a human force specification.

- Puddle & Maulder2013 compared10traceurs' landings from0.75m. Precision and roll reduced peak vertical force/loading rate relative to the traditional condition; precision versus roll differences were not significant. This supports prepared flexion and finite recovery, **not** an arbitrary damage-discount percentage. [Original paper](https://www.jssm.org/volume12/iss1/cap/jssm-12-122.pdf).
- Maldonado etal2018 found longer absorption duration and greater lower-limb motion in trained precision landings. Map to contact-dependent compression/recovery and input preparation; do not erase incoming velocity. [Original study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02640414.2018.1469226).
- Croft, Schroeder & Bertram2019 describe the horizontal-to-vertical wall-climb transition as a trade-off between positive/negative leg work, with an intermediate useful approach speed. Faster is not universally better. [Study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30446542/).
- Lawson2015 measured ground, wall-foot and hand contributions in20participants. Wall contact supplies vertical frictional reaction. Map wall-running to finite traction-limited foot plants, not gravity cancellation. [Original thesis](https://apexmovement.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Vertical_Wall_Run_Research_Paper.pdf).
- Maldonado etal2020's static-start kong uses overlapping feet/hands then hands-only support and flight. Its timing does not prescribe a running kong. [Original study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10255842.2020.1714932).
- Michilsens etal2012 distinguishes overlapping-hand continuous-contact brachiation from ricochetal flight. Use independent hand generations and preserve release velocity; do not transplant ape capabilities. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3507214/).
- Coaching distinguishes supported step vaults, short-hand-contact speed vaults and prepared wall-foot/edge catches. [Step vault](https://www.exploreparkour.com/linear-step-vault/), [speed vault](https://www.parkour.org/moves-blog/2018/7/26/speed-vault), [cat-leap feet](https://londonparkour.com/tutorials/how-to-cat-leap-foot-position-2/), [cat-leap arms](https://londonparkour.com/tutorials/how-to-cat-leap-arm-preparedness/).
- [LAAS-CNRS motion/force database](https://gepettoweb.laas.fr/parkour/) supplies contact-order and presentation reference for vaults/pull-ups/muscle-ups. Root motion is reference only, never movement authority.

Research supports the mechanism and sequence, not universal numeric human limits. All new durations/speeds below are explicit tuning hypotheses to be exercised over ranges. Existing force/work laws retain their current values unless a separately evidenced owner repair is necessary.

## Shared inputs

Keep current named verbs and device bindings. Move/Look remain live throughout transitions. Jump is a fresh press; held movement is intent, never an automatic repeated jump. Action chooses a reachable grip/operation; Drop deliberately releases. Sprint is Shift/pad stick-click/touch outer-stick extension. Crouch is existing Ctrl-held/C-toggle/pad stick-click/touch toggle. Existing Jump/Action/Drop controls retain equipment and machine precedence. No new required multi-button chord on touch.

Context: Jump on legal foot contact requests takeoff; Jump on a contacted wall requests wall push; Jump in a hand-supported hang requests physical top-out or existing deliberate release when aimed away. Action in air requests a validated catch; Action in a one-hand hang requests a separately reachable missing hand/regrip; with two compatible hanging hands it requests the same physical top-out as Jump. Crouch while running requests slide; Crouch prepared before a sufficiently energetic directional landing requests compact recovery/roll. Explicit Drop cancels pending acquisition and buffered traversal; it never makes a missed command fire later.

Store anchors in actual support/material frames. A fresh input transaction may choose one plan; do not repeatedly hijack held intent into different moves. Three reusable16-particle emitters cap active powder at48particles, without shadows, collision or gameplay force. World-space particles stay behind the moving player. Startup/rewind/death seed quiet receipt baselines; motion settings and pause stop active powder; disabled or airborne receipts are consumed without delayed bursts. Actual frame cost and phone appearance remain device observations, not inferred from the cap.

Every exit leaves the body in the solver's state. Destroy constraints outside Update. Restart/death clears transient plans, buffers/contact generations and visual responses. Supported top-out only detaches completed hands while preserving a valid finite foot push. Loss of a foot may return to an actual hang, loss of one hand may retain the other, loss of all contacts yields ordinary flight.
