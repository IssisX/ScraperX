# Sound license audit

Every audio file under `godot/` (176: 39 Matron recordings, 57 fall-voice clips, 40 story-radio clips, 40 story-radio source wavs; no `.mp3`) checked against `godot/presentation/ui/licences.gd`, `godot/presentation/audio/matron/CREDITS.md`, `godot/audio/story_radio/{CREDITS.md,VOICE_LICENSES.txt,KOKORO_LICENSE.txt,manifest.json}` and the generator headers in `src/voice/`. `tools/fab/sources.json` and `tools/body/sources.json` hold no sound. Read-only except the credit fix below; no sound file was touched, no license was guessed (every license here is quoted from a record in the repo).

Bottom line: **100 of the 136 shipped sounds are fine** (CC0 or project-generated). **36 are flagged** (Kokoro takes: not CC0, not the project's own generator). 40 story-radio clips are loaded by nothing. The in-game LICENCES page was missing the credit for the Matron's 39 recordings and the story radio; that is fixed in this branch.

## 1. Missing credits

- **M1 (fixed).** The in-game LICENCES page (`licences.gd`) said "Every other sound ... generated by the project's own code", but the Matron's 39 sounds are recordings. They are credited per file in `matron/CREDITS.md` (CC0 1.0, source URL, author, title) and nowhere in the game. Added `HER_SOUNDS_NOTE` with the 22 authors named in that file's table.
- **M2 (fixed).** The page said the fall voice has 47 lines; `src/voice/generate_fall_voice.py` writes 57, `voice_lines.gd` lists 57 and 57 files ship. Changed to 57. (The 47 dates from 2026-09-28, `00_START_HERE_ARCHIVE.md`.) Revert this one line if the count should stay as the owner last saw it; the other two fixes stand alone.
- **M3 (fixed).** The story radio's lines are not on the page. Added `RADIO_NOTE` (Kokoro-82M Apache-2.0; Chatterbox TTS MIT with Piper en_US-ljspeech-high and en_US-bryce-medium reference voices, public-domain training data), all from `story_radio/CREDITS.md`.
- Not fixable here: none of the sound files is individually credited on the in-game page (the fall voice and the story radio are credited as groups); the repo's own plan says "Credits (who made the game) wait on the owner" (`RELEASE_PLAN.md`).

## 2. License problems (flagged: not CC0 and not the project's own generator, or not yet verified)

- **L1. 36 Kokoro-82M story-radio takes** (everything in `story_radio/` except the 4 remade files): Apache-2.0 model output (`KOKORO_LICENSE.txt`), produced through a third-party public hosted Space (`Pendrokar/Kokoro-TTS`, revision recorded in `manifest.json`), "hosted weights revision: not exposed by hosted API", and the Kokoro voices' entries in `manifest.json` say "no exclusive rights or clearance beyond published model terms are asserted". `story_radio/CREDITS.md` adds that these takes are not allowed by the owner's rule on voices and "stay gated off". They are gated off in play (nothing loads them, list 3) but they still ship: the export filter is `tests/*, tools/*` only (`export_presets.cfg`). Files: arrival_iona_empty_record, arrival_iona_first_ring, arrival_iona_mesh_lip, arrival_iona_personnel_way, double_ari_before, double_ari_cannot, double_ari_dev_has_scope, double_ari_job, double_ari_original_scope, double_ari_told, double_ari_will, double_iona_here, double_ro_and_face, double_ro_dev_has_scope, double_ro_empty, double_ro_face, double_ro_name, double_ro_original_scope, double_ro_when, double_ro_who, freight_one_iona_crown, handover_dev_received, handover_iona_ari_first, handover_iona_gallery, handover_iona_revision, handover_record_assurance, handover_ro_ari_first, handover_ro_denial, handover_ro_send_scope, handover_ro_witness, handover_source_survey, receiving_iona_come_look, service_iona_inspection_rail, service_iona_swing_stair, vale_contact_iona, vale_contact_ro.
- **L2. The 40 `story_radio/source/*.wav`** are the clean sources of those clips and of the 4 remade ones. `source/.gdignore` keeps them out of the import and the export, so they do not ship; they sit in the repo under the same terms as their ogg.
- **L3. 33 of the 39 Matron files rest on a mirror's metadata for CC0.** `matron/CREDITS.md`: "Where a Freesound recording was fetched from a mirror, the mirror's metadata gives its license as CC0 1.0 (the Freesound page itself was not reachable from the build machine)": the LAION Freesound set or FSD50K. The license column is CC0 1.0 for every row (69 source rows, all CC0), so by the rule they are fine, but only 6 files (matron_effort_2, matron_plant_0, matron_plant_1, matron_shriek_4, matron_shriek_5, matron_swallow_1) rest wholly on a source's own page (OpenGameArt). Check each Freesound page before release.
- **L4. 61 project-generated voice files have an open clearance.** The fall voice (57) and the 4 remade story lines are generated offline by the project's scripts (Chatterbox TTS MIT, Piper reference voices on public-domain data, Whisper MIT as a check), which the rule counts as the project's own. `licences.gd` itself records "the clearance of these is release plan item 18, open", and Chatterbox marks its output with an inaudible Perth watermark (`story_radio/CREDITS.md`). Fine for a commercial game on the recorded terms; listed so the clearance is not forgotten. The one voice the records say is not cleared is Dev's (Piper's `en_US-lessac`, Blizzard 2013 data): no Piper voice of his is shipped, his line is one of the Kokoro takes in L1.

## 3. Orphaned files and credits

- **O1. 40 story-radio oggs: nothing loads them.** Their only reader is `presentation/audio/story_radio_player.gd` (via `manifest.json`); no `.gd`, `.tscn`, `project.godot` or export file mentions that script (`grep story_radio` over `godot/` finds only the script and the manifest). The merge commit says the voices are "gated until the recordings are approved". They are in the export all the same.
- **O2. 40 `source/*.wav`:** not loaded by design, excluded from import (L2).
- **O3. Credits naming files that no longer exist: none.** `matron/CREDITS.md` names 39 files, all 39 exist and there are no others; `story_radio/CREDITS.md` names 4, all exist; `manifest.json` names 40 cues and 40 oggs exist, one to one with the 40 wavs; `voice_lines.gd` lists the 57 fall-voice files, all exist and there are no others.
- **O4. Records that point elsewhere.** `matron/CREDITS.md` cites `03_EXECUTION/wip_20261027/her_real/` and its `provenance.csv` "on the wip-notes branch": that path is not on trunk; the branch exists (`claude/wip-notes-20261027`), and the credits table is complete without it.
- Not orphaned: all 39 Matron clips are loaded by `sound_bank.gd` `build_her()` (`HER_DIR` + `<clip>_<take>.ogg`, takes per `HER_RECORDED`) and each of the 11 clip names is used by her sound code; all 57 fall-voice clips are in `voice_lines.gd`, which `audio_director.gd` preloads.

## What changed

`godot/presentation/ui/licences.gd`: the fall-voice count 47 to 57, the sentence "Every other sound ... own code" moved to its own constant, and two new constants for the Matron's recordings and the story radio, all joined under the page's SOUND heading. Checked: `licences.gd` loads under Godot 4.7 and `Licences.text()` prints the four notes (run with `--headless --script`); the `touch_pause` scenario requires "Chatterbox" on that page, still present. The scenario itself was not run (CI runs it on this push).
