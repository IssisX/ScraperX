# Adversarial ideation

Produce a compelling, understandable idea that survives useful attempts to disprove it. Root owns the judgment and synthesis; neither agent count nor agreement establishes quality. This is a reusable idea-development method across domains, not a ScraperX engineering profile or another delivery workflow.

## Offer it at a useful decision

The owner wants an invitation before choosing this workflow. Suggest it when materially different approaches remain open and comparison could prevent expensive wrong work, or when a major creative feature needs stronger originality and feasibility. Ask briefly: “Want to use adversarial ideation for this? Independent designs, then critics who try to break them.” Explain the expected benefit if it is not obvious.

An explicit `$adversarial-ideation` invocation or explicit request for independent authors and adversarial agents already selects the method; do not ask again. Do not offer it for routine edits, straightforward fixes, each subtask of an accepted plan, or merely because agents are available. Re-offer only at a new consequential choice, not repeatedly while the same invitation is pending or after it was declined. Use an available asynchronous question tool for the optional choice and continue useful independent authorized work. Silence is not selection; preserve the current task and paused work.

After selection, run the bounded process below without further method-confirmation loops. A concept request authorizes a concept, not its implementation or publication outside existing permissions.

## Ground the common brief

Reuse accepted facts. When an idea depends on an existing system, locate only its relevant authoritative owners, current revision, interfaces, constraints and prior comparable work. A mapper is useful when those facts are missing; it is not an automatic role. For other domains, identify the equivalent primary artifacts and facts rather than inventing a code audit.

Freeze a common brief containing:

- Desired user experience and useful finish: starting situation, action, consequence, onward use and failure/recovery where relevant.
- Hard constraints separately from preferences, authorized scope, exclusions and existing solutions that would make a proposal repetitive.
- What actually exists, what is unknown, resource limits and source references. Proposed APIs, unmeasured parameters and past notes are not implemented capabilities.
- The few evaluation dimensions that matter to this decision, with comparable meaning and anchors. Examples: user value/originality, causal soundness, usability, constraint fit and feasible delivery. Adapt these to the domain.
- Expected candidate format and what evidence could change the selection.

Do not slip a favored mechanism into one author's brief. Send consequential factual corrections to every author, recording what changed.

## Independent authors, then fresh refuters

Use the smallest team that can supply real independent alternatives and useful challenges. A substantial round might use two or three authors followed by two or three critics; those are examples, not minimums or staffing targets. Local agents and MCP model advisers share the same capacity and resource budget. Use waves when needed. Read-only assignments are the default for ideation; avoid shared writers and recursive delegations.

Each assignment states objective, common evidence/revision, owned surface, dependencies, output, stopping condition and actual supported model/effort. Match effort to uncertainty and consequence; routine mapping is cheaper than coupled design or causal critique. Preserve configured models and safeguards. Do not claim a prompt changed runtime effort or launch costlier models merely for prestige.

Authors work in fresh contexts with the same brief. They do not see peer proposals, favored answers or verdicts until their candidate is complete. Do not full-history-fork peers' ideas into an allegedly independent assignment. They may inspect primary evidence within scope. If separate contexts are unavailable, disclose that the alternatives are a solo exercise; do not manufacture independence.

Each author returns one bounded candidate with:

- A plain-language pitch and concrete interaction sequence, including first use, consequences, recovery and completion.
- Its causal mechanism, existing owners/capabilities versus new work, resource/dependency costs and strongest reason to reject it.
- Important assumptions, one discriminating calculation or counterexample where useful, and the cheapest observation that would decide the central uncertainty. Label numbers as chosen, derived, sourced or measured.
- Comparable scores on the brief's dimensions, short reasons, confidence and evidence limits. Scores are design judgments, not measured outcomes.

Freeze the candidates before critique. Give fresh critics the same candidates and common evidence; withhold peer verdicts and root's preferred winner. Assign different relevant lenses, such as causal/physical correctness, human interaction/originality, and implementation/lifecycle/resource cost. Domain-specific concerns replace these examples when appropriate. Several identical skeptics add less value than complementary questions.

A critic tries to kill the claim, not merely improve the prose. Require a concrete counterexample, caller-to-failure path, violated contract, equation or conflicting primary fact. They must distinguish:

- **REFUTED:** a supported contradiction or failure path. State whether it kills the central premise or only a chosen parameter/detail.
- **UNPROVEN:** a material assumption lacks the observation needed to settle it. Uncertainty is not automatically refutation.
- **PLAUSIBLE:** the idea survived this scoped analysis. This does not mean implemented, tested, safe, performant or enjoyable.

Keep critic output comparable: `candidate; status; claim challenged; evidence and confidence; consequence; smallest honest repair; deciding observation; scores with reasons`. Subjective dislike and confirmed defects are different findings. Source inspection, analytic estimates, runtime observations and human preferences support different claims.

## Synthesize through judgment

Root checks the consequential claims against the actual evidence and resolves disagreement. Do not count votes as proof, average away a hard constraint, or trust a critic simply because its job was to refute. Multiple same-model judgments can share a blind spot. One confirmed fatal defect outweighs several favorable ratings; absence of a found defect proves only the limited search performed.

Choose the best expected user outcome, not automatically the cheapest prototype, highest self-score or most theatrical pitch. Borrow a losing design's useful component only when its interfaces, causal mechanism and constraints fit; identify any changed scope and added risks. A synthesis that changes the central mechanism is a new untested claim. Seek only the affected additional analysis or observation when it could change selection, not another automatic full review chain.

There need not be a winner. If every proposal fails, explain the shared blocker and choose a materially different framing or the most useful bounded experiment within authorization. Do not crown a broken design to make the process look productive.

## Finish without an endless tournament

Stop when root can justify the choice, name the decisive remaining uncertainty and identify the next useful action. Another critique round needs a specific new claim, evidence gap or materially changed proposal that could alter that decision. Repeated prose, unchanged scores and finding nothing new are not reasons to keep going. There is no universal “attack until dry,” fixed round quota or mandatory two-empty-round hunt.

When a prototype is authorized, define an observation that could reject the premise, its keep/change/kill criterion and its bounded outcome before building polish. Do not turn repeated failures into parameter sweeps or replace missing gameplay with favorable agent scores. Preserve real implementation/release gates; use the existing delivery workflow and matching evidence rather than adding another process stack.

Deliver the selected idea in human terms, why alternatives lost, material corrections, unresolved claims and the one next observation that matters. Save the decision and resumption conditions in the existing task record, linking detailed artifacts only where useful. Distinguish a reviewed concept from delivered behavior. Leave unrelated paused implementation paused.
