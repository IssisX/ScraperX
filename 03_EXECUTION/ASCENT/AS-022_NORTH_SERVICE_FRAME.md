# AS-022 north service frame

**Player problem.** Cross from the earned +88 m tower ring to its north service frame, climb and traverse sideways to the +99 m ring, then climb to a west shelf that requires a jump and hanging catch. A low canopy blocks immediate standing top-out at the catch; shimmy north to the open pocket, finish a short climb, and return by a high catwalk and mantle to the existing +110 m ring. The exposed catches can be released and retried on real rests. This is a climbing and parkour section between AS-020's adjustable machine and later machinery, not another automatic vertical ride.
