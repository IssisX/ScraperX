# Active goal: supplied machines, challenging ascent and human-limited parkour

## Scope and authority

This is the full execution scope of the owner's active goal, including the
movement, obstacle, feedback and material-quality additions made in this

objective. That short record does not remove any requirement below. The

**Objective:** Turn the supplied machine designs into varied, physically causal
ascent gameplay, prioritizing weak existing route connections and completing
one playable integration at a time. Develop the responsive, human-limited,
momentum-preserving movement and deliberately designed parkour needed to use
those machines and connect their approaches, exits and recoveries.

The player's intent requests an action. Actual physical state determines
whether and how it succeeds. Animation, camera, sound and vibration communicate
the result; consequential native physics produces it.

## 3. World experience and encounter design

The owner added this direction to the existing machine-and-parkour goal at the
next safe ownership boundary. It develops current authoring rather than replacing
the goal or launching an unrelated world redesign. Preserve completed gameplay
and ongoing work.

The player is a capable, human-limited climber inside an enormous working
industrial structure. They study it, take risks, manipulate it and exploit its
physical behavior to climb. Experience turns frightening places into understood
places, building confidence and mastery. Seek awe at scale and bodily presence;
curiosity about routes/consequences; apprehension before commitment; urgency in
demanding motion; human panic, recovery and relief; satisfaction from understanding
or a legitimate alternative; and ownership of the player's changes.

Working mechanisms alone do not prove a convincing place, meaningful choices or
emotional pacing. Machinery/movement outpacing their surroundings is a design
hypothesis grounded in inspected source/documents, not verified phone feedback.

1. **Distinct places with understandable purposes.** Give regions recognizable
   industrial functions and identities through coherent geometry, machinery,
   lighting, sound, material and selective human traces. Freight, maintenance,
   processing, crane work and occupied pockets are examples, not a mandatory
   roster. Bound regions by useful geometry/function. Players should remember
   what happens there, beyond an elevation or machine name.
2. **Meaningful decisions.** Vary noticing, inference, manipulation and execution.
   Different devices still repeat if every encounter is board/UP/wait/exit. Make
   choices about position, load, alignment, restraint, timing and route matter.
   Preserve physically legitimate alternatives, including occasional exposed
   shortcuts versus longer sheltered approaches with genuine tradeoffs.
3. **Neighboring encounters.** Where useful, machine operation repositions a useful
   load, exposes a crossing, clears an obstruction, earns access or makes a return
   route as well as changing altitude. Keep consequences visible and persistent.
   Each additional stage must add a decision, consequence or traversal opportunity.
4. **Tension and relief through space.** Author sheltered observation, anticipation,
   exposed commitment, demanding execution and secure arrival where appropriate.
   Vary rhythm through enclosure, views, sound, footing and exposure so height
   registers emotionally. Near misses come from real physics; recovery needs a
   physically feasible player action.
5. **Convincing human presence.** Selective repairs, maintenance access, stored
   supplies, occupied spaces and appropriate activity should explain use/history.
   Workers/operators are justified when they add meaningful interaction and obey
   actual access/world state. Begin with coherent places, not a population framework.
6. **Reasons to explore and continue.** Reward exploration with useful knowledge,
   equipment access, shortcuts, alternative approaches and views that reveal
   machinery. Show progress through changed surroundings, recognizable landmarks,
   earned access and growing understanding. **Why the summit matters is an open
   design decision; invented backstory is not established canon.**

For each current encounter or connected place, resolve its purpose; what the
approach teaches; the meaningful choice; the actual physical state changed; the
movement skill exploiting that change; the rise/release of tension; the result of
a miss and feasible recovery; the useful persistent aftermath; and how the exit
prepares the next experience. Complete this depth, then move onward.

Prioritize place, decisions and emotional pacing. Add detail when it strengthens
those outcomes. For the important gravity-fed wheel, author the whole approach,
observation, finite material feeding, boarding decisions, exposed ascent,
challenging exit, failure/recovery and persistent aftermath, with deliberately
high-quality geometry/material/contact/audio/retention/spill behavior. Falling
material physically drives the wheel.

## 4. Challenging parkour and human-limited movement

Design and build the obstacles as primary gameplay work. The player must read
the structure, think, take risks, climb, traverse, activate and use machines to
earn progress. Avoid making every solution obvious. Include physically earned
near misses, recoverable loss of balance and missed catches with coherent
consequences/recovery rather than random animation or hidden rescue forces.

Build a unified model for running, sprinting, jumping, climbing, mantling,
vaulting, hanging, swing pumping, timed release, ledge catching, impact bracing,
deliberate rolls, stumbling and balance. Fill missing pieces coherently rather
than treating the owner's examples as a complete inventory. Aim for smooth,
responsive, visceral and tactile movement with agile human limitations.

### Ground movement and air control

- Request movement velocity through input, but earn changes through available
  traction, contact normal/load, material friction, actuator capacity and time.
- Use actual support contact-point velocity, including angular motion. Preserve
  inertia when accelerating, reversing, braking or changing direction.
- Preserve genuine excess world momentum from moving supports, swings or
  launchers. The sprint ceiling is not a global velocity clamp. Zero input
  brakes through real friction/effort on the ground and does not cancel an
  airborne arc.
- Keep air control responsive but weaker than ground control, with bounded
  force/work and no arbitrary lateral velocity replacement. Preserve currently
  successful acceleration, sprint feel, jump reach and routes until comparison
  demonstrates an improvement.

### Jump, catch, climb and release

- Jump only through valid physical support, using finite push-off impulse/work.
  Dynamic receivers receive the equal-and-opposite reaction at the actual
  contact point, accounting for effective inertia and rotation. Carry existing
  horizontal and support-point world velocity into departure.
- Buffer intent briefly in native ticks if needed; execute only after valid
  contact. Recent departure from an edge is not a free-air jump permission.
- Retain independent finite-force physical hands. A catch requires real
  reachable, unobstructed geometry, sustainable support/load, acceptable closing
  velocity, available hand force and actual constraint/collision engagement.
- Climb through reachable regrips and controlled work, including one-handed
  load redistribution and genuine rotating/moving/deforming support motion.
  Do not add rider weight twice. Slip/fall when reach, force or geometry fails.
- Use compliant catches to absorb load over time. Fast arrivals can swing or
  slip. No teleporting, magnetizing, instant momentum removal or automatic
  reacquisition of an intentionally released grip.
- Passive release removes constraints on the authoritative tick and preserves
  actual world velocity. A powered departure is a separate explicit finite-work
  action with an actual reaction path. Flight remains ballistic with legitimate
  air-control forces; arrival depends on phase, speed, geometry and reach.

### Physical mantles, vaults and swings

- Convert one demonstrated gravity-off/root-velocity-prescribed mantle/vault
  path at a time. Preserve geometry/clearance, receiving-footing proof and the
  repaired1.7s production mantle. A replacement must remain responsive.
- Earn transfers through actual hand attachment/reaction, finite pull-up work,
  available foot contact/push-off and continuous collision-honest arrival.
  Preserve support motion and approach/exit momentum; do not reset speed.
- Swing through real player mass, suspended structure, constraints and gravity.
  Pump with finite physically represented body/extension/COM changes. Correct
  timing can add energy; poor timing can remove energy or accomplish little;
  neutral pumping adds no energy beyond external sources. No arbitrary angular
  velocity increment or automatic phase selection in gameplay.
- Record supplied work separately from storage, delivered mechanical exchange,
  dissipation and numerical residual when evaluating energy closure. A command
  bound alone is not complete physical energy accounting.

### Impact, deliberate roll, stumble and balance

- Keep existing relative-energy landing recovery as the baseline. Use actual
  normal/tangent approach speed, contact impulse, slip and available support for
  finite bracing. Prepared and late/poorly oriented landings must differ in
  physically observable contact/deceleration and future movement.
- A roll requires deliberate preparation/input, suitable footing, compatible
  forward velocity and actual clear runout. It consumes real distance/time,
  carries appropriate momentum, collides with surrounding geometry and changes
  the distribution of deceleration. Capsule tuck plus animation alone is not
  anatomical or physical rolling proof. A reduced-order roll must implement and
  identify its contact, impulse exchange, energy loss, travel and limitations.
- Preserve the native20m/s lethal-impact contract. Rolling must not erase damage
  or evade death. A threshold change requires a separate gameplay decision.
- Cause instability through off-axis impact, tangential slip, obstructed motion,
  inadequate traction, sudden support acceleration or narrow/rotating footing.
  Recovery needs contact and finite input-directed correction/stepping within
  reach. Mild errors allow a playable correction; severe ones can fall. Do not
  substitute random stumbling, fixed input lockouts or invisible rescue forces.
- Preserve narrow-beam gameplay and account for real support footprint, COM and
  relative horizontal velocity. Use the reduced-order XCoM approximation
  `COM + horizontal_velocity / sqrt(g / effective_COM_height)` where appropriate;
  it is not a full anatomical model. Countersteering or stepping requires feasible
  contact and effort, and missing support leads to ordinary falling.

## 5. Controls, native ownership and feedback

- Keep consequential simulation under existing native C++17/Jolt ownership at
  90Hz. Keep Godot input/presentation and render interpolation. Do not replace
  the dynamic player with `CharacterVirtual` or create another physics authority.
- Preserve existing85kg mass,5.5m/s movement,8m/s sprint,5.5m/s upward jump,
  existing22/14m/s² controller constants,1500N/hand ceiling and3000W default hand
  command budget. These are engine parameters, not universal physiological
  constants. Reuse real hand coupling, contact recovery, dynamic reactions,
  native snapshots and interpolation. Use the pinned Jolt APIs.
- Preserve keyboard/controller/touch vocabulary. Native contextual arbitration:
  supported Jump pushes off; valid Hang+Jump requests mantle/contextual action;
  Action near a reachable moving hold attempts catch; Drop intentionally releases;
  directional hang input climbs/traverses/pumps as actual geometry allows;
  Crouch/prepared input requests viable bracing/rolling. Promptly recognize intent;
  force/inertia still determine the motion. Avoid ambiguous extra touch buttons.
- Keep short tick-based buffers for intent only. Contact, geometry and energy
  cannot be manufactured by buffering. Transition on native ticks independently
  of render frame rate. Do not add decorative stamina/fatigue meters.
- Extend existing native-state-driven camera/body/audio/haptic consumers. Communicate
  grip engagement, sustained load/strain, impulse-scaled heavy landing, slips and
  balance corrections, mantle weight transfer, release/free flight and recovering
  gait. Coalesce by native tick and throttle vibration. Keep camera motion bounded
  and avoid drift/double interpolation. Honor vibration, audio and reduced-motion
  settings. Android permission/device support and actual phone feel are separate
  evidence from API availability.
